How it works

  1. Connect your wallet on BNB Smart Chain (chain 56).
  2. We check on-chain that your wallet holds an Agent ID (balanceOf on the Agent ID contract). No sign-in, no QR code, no session stored anywhere.
  3. Tap Mint and confirm the transaction in your own wallet. Mint is free — gas only.
  4. Come back daily and check in to grow your streak.
